# TenderToFit Fresh Constitution

## Mission
Help a consulting organization make a safer, faster, evidence-backed decision about whether to commit scarce bid resources to a live opportunity.

## Positioning
**Bid Decision Intelligence**, not AI tender writing.

## Core truth hierarchy
1. Tender source text and official documents.
2. Human-reviewed requirement records with provenance.
3. Verified organization evidence.
4. Human commercial judgment.
5. Derived summaries.

Derived outputs never outrank source evidence.

## Decision laws
- A mandatory `NOT_HELD` requirement is a blocker.
- A mandatory `UNKNOWN`, `PARTIAL`, or unverified item keeps compliance unresolved.
- The system does not infer that silence means compliance.
- A `BID` record is rejected unless compliance is `READY_FOR_HUMAN_DECISION`.
- No recommendation is final without human sign-off.
- Commercial factors are not converted into pseudo-scientific win probabilities.

## Commercial validation laws
- Do not count free demos as demand.
- Record the real price and whether it was actually paid.
- Record baseline assessment time and actual delivery/review time.
- Record whether the diagnostic changed the client's decision.
- Record material risks that the existing process missed.
- A second paid diagnostic is stronger evidence than positive feedback.
- Benchmark against generic AI + Excel on the same tender whenever possible.

## Privacy laws
- Store only data required for the pilot.
- Do not send confidential tender/company evidence to a model provider automatically.
- PDF extraction is local in this build.
- Cloud deployment requires a separate privacy/security gate.

## Scope lock
The pilot ends at bid-decision intelligence and an auditable compliance matrix.
It does not expand to proposal writing, CRM, automated tender discovery, submission automation, financing, consortium marketplace, or enterprise workflow until paid repeat behavior is demonstrated.

## Kill gates
Freeze or pivot the standalone product if the pilot shows any of the following:
- no meaningful willingness to pay;
- little repeat purchase;
- generic AI + Excel performs nearly as well;
- decision support rarely changes or protects decisions;
- organization onboarding cost is excessive;
- confidentiality prevents usable deployment;
- value is primarily analyst service rather than self-service software.

A managed Bid Decision Intelligence service is an acceptable outcome. It is not considered product failure if customers pay repeatedly for the outcome while software remains internal infrastructure.
