1. Understanding of the assignment
The assignment requires strengthening secondary education data systems through diagnostic review, stakeholder consultation, institutional assessment, roadmap development, and capacity building.

2. Technical approach
The proposed approach combines education system analysis, data governance review, institutional process mapping, M&E framework assessment, and costed roadmap development.

3. Workstream 1: Inception and diagnostic framework
Confirm scope, stakeholders, data sources, deliverable expectations, and assessment tools.

4. Workstream 2: Education data system diagnostic
Review existing data flows, reporting systems, institutional responsibilities, data quality, and bottlenecks.

5. Workstream 3: Roadmap and capacity-building plan
Develop phased recommendations, training modules, governance actions, and implementation roadmap.

6. Quality assurance
Use document review, stakeholder validation, internal peer review, and deliverable quality checklist.
